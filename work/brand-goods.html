Holy Card Series — Pencil on Paper

  • Illustration
  • Pencil Drawing
  • Brand Goods
  • Packaging Art

“Rise every morning. Die every night.”

— the daily spirituality of a Catholic monastic order

A series of holy cards drawn with a pencil — the simplest, most everyday tool — to hold one quiet idea: live only today, and close it in peace.

01. Concept

Living in the present

Not worrying about tomorrow in advance, not bound to yesterday — living this one day, and only this day, completely.

A peaceful ending

By practicing death each night, the fear of the real ending fades, and every moment can rest in gratitude and peace.

02. Material — Why a Pencil

It is the most ordinary tool we own — the one we reach for every day, the one that can be erased and begun again. Yet layer by layer, graphite turns a plain sheet into light, shadow and depth. Simple, familiar, and deep: the same qualities I wanted the cards to carry.

03. Practice

Drawing is presence

In the moment of drawing there is only the line in front of me. The work itself becomes a way of being fully here — no yesterday, no tomorrow.

Finishing is a small ending

Completing each card closes the day with order and calm: a tidy, quiet full stop before rest.

04. Sharing

Printed as small cards, the drawings travel from hand to hand — an everyday object that carries a reminder to live today fully.

Today, fully. Tonight, gently.
