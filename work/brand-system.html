A family of Hiossen marketing materials built on one brand guideline — applied consistently across corporate, clinician and patient touchpoints.

01. Corporate & Brand

Corporate Brochure: The master brand narrative — heritage, global footprint, quality control and digital workflow — establishing the color, typography and grid every other piece follows.

System Rules: Signature red as the action color, deep navy for clinical authority, generous white space and a consistent product-photography style.

02. Clinician-facing (B2B)

Campaign Series — ET IV: One implant, four clinical messages (immediate loading, soft bone, cortical preservation, surface) sharing one dark, high-contrast layout so the series reads as a set.

Surgical Kit Line: A modular flyer system for CAS, LAS, OneCAS, OneEM and OssBuilder kits — consistent hero product, color-coded titles and an identical information hierarchy.

Audience-Specific Messaging: Separate GP and Specialist pieces, product brochures for the EK system and biomaterials, and SureGenix product packaging.

03. Patient-facing

Multilingual Patient Leaflet: One tri-fold localized into English, Spanish and Chinese for diverse U.S. practices — layout and hierarchy preserved, language and typography adapted.

Implant Passport: A pocket-sized record patients keep after treatment — the brand's most personal, long-lasting touchpoint.
