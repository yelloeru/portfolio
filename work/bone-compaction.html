Bone Compaction Kit Launch Campaign

Product Launch — Visual & Launch Strategy

Driving a 4x increase in engagement through a high-impact 3D motion video campaign.

  • Motion Design
  • Plan & Strategy
  • Social Media

01. Context & Strategy

Need: Clinicians needed the kit, and the launch needed a teaser to build anticipation before the reveal.

Challenge: Lack of a formal launch framework initially led to simple static graphic proposals — and no 3D video assets were ready.

Insight: Competitive analysis revealed short-form video consistently outperforms static posts in capturing engagement.

Strategy: Shifted to a video-first, curiosity-driven teaser approach — briefly revealing the drill silhouette before introducing core functional advantages.

02. Execution

Teaser Video: A silhouette-only teaser that withheld the product to spark curiosity.

Main Launch Video: After several iterations, delivered the final motion video walking through the 4 key features of the kit.

03. Outcome & Business Impact

4x

Engagement — the teaser reached four times the usual engagement with no paid advertising.

Successful launch: Shared positive KOL case studies at the conference launch, and sales increased.

Lasting reference: The campaign was later retired for reasons outside marketing, but remains an internal reference for launch best practice.
