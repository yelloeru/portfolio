About

Jung H. Lee, Brand & MarCom Lead

I’m a designer who wants to give people room to breathe.

Everyone is busy. Even when we rest, we rest by watching something loud.

I like to look at the world and its people from a little distance — the memories left where someone has just passed, the thoughts that keep coming back, and the spaces I fill with imagination. Through words and visuals, I want to share that quiet pleasure.

How I Work

01

Difference comes from time, not decoration.

I don't believe a difference has to come from heavy styling. It lives in accumulated time — a space where someone has worked for years, worn and familiar objects, and the stories that rub off on them.

02

Capture what words can't.

Some things are hard to put into words. I look for collaborators who sense that atmosphere — the quiet, concentrated energy of someone fully absorbed in their work.

03

Relationships beyond the brief.

We may meet as a company and a client, but the bond often grows deeper. Sharing even a brief moment of that story is the most rewarding part of what I do.
